Lease years run from the phase 1 handover on 15 January (Y1 = 15 Jan 2027 - 14 Jan 2028). Each phase carries its own fit-out period (head rent after its rent-free months and pre-opening payroll with no revenue) and its own occupancy ramp. Leasing commissions of 10% of contracted annual rent fall in each phase's trading years when member leases originate or renew (3-year cycle). Core operating costs (payroll, utilities, pantry, maintenance, insurance) are the Sky Tower Floor 38 budget scaled to each phase's area from its opening, escalating 3% a year.

Method and assumptions. Monthly engine, six units on floor 24 of Sky Tower (2,146.44 sqm, 23,104 sq ft per title deeds), all of it rentable suite area and run as one serviced-office operation, taken in two phases on one direct head lease. Phase 1 - 2401, 2404, 2405, 2406 (1,518.60 sqm) - hands over on 15 January 2027 and starts the 10-year term. Phase 2 - 2402, 2403 (627.84 sqm) - hands over 2 months later (scenario A, 15 March 2027) or 12 months later at the end of the sitting tenant's 1-year lease (scenario B, 15 January 2028), and expires with the main lease. Head rent AED 2,000/sqm in lease year 1 on all units, 5% annual escalation on each 15 January anniversary, 3 months rent-free from each phase's handover; service charge included in the head rent; security deposit 25% of first-year rent per phase, returned at term end. No compensation to any outgoing tenant. Fit-out AED 550/sq ft plus 5% contingency, 7 months per phase; 5% VAT on fit-out recovered the following year. Legal and company set-up AED 120,000 once at signing; broker commission 10% of first-year head rent per phase, paid at each phase's handover; no separate launch marketing budget - launch marketing is delivered through the pre-launch consulting. Phase 1 costs are funded at closing; phase 2 fit-out, deposit, broker fee and marketing are called at the phase 2 handover. Two months of pre-opening payroll per phase. Each phase opens at 60% of capacity desks, rising 5 points a month to stabilisation; 1% credit loss. Capacity 70 sq ft of suite area per desk. Core operating costs are the Sky Tower Floor 38 budget (AED 105,100 a month for 23,000 sq ft) scaled pro rata to each phase's area, 3% escalation; ADGM licence AED 50,000 a year. Leasing commission 10% of contracted annual rent on origination and each 3-year renewal. Operator: 5% management fee on collected revenue, then a 70/30 investor/operator split of each year's positive NOI after fee (no loss carry-forward); the investor funds any negative year through a working-capital reserve sized at closing. Acquisition, launch and sales fees: acquisition consulting fee 5% of each phase's first-year head rent (AED 214,644 for the full floor) paid in November 2026 (phase 1) and December 2026 (phase 2); pre-launch consulting AED 200,000 lump sum in November 2026; launch consulting AED 250,000 lump sum in February 2027; performance-based sales expenses - up to AED 550,000 pro rata to desks under signed leases 2 months after opening, and up to AED 500,000 on the weighted average lease term, paid quarterly in months 13-24 after opening; funded by the investor in the base case. Annual cash flows; returns are pre-tax and unlevered; no residual value at lease expiry.
