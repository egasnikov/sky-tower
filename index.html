Risk factors

Every risk has a mitigant

Click each risk to see how it's addressed.

Occupancy ramp slower than projected▼

Conservative base case assumes 50% Y1 occupancy with gradual ramp. Break-even at ~49% occupancy. ADGM's mandatory office requirement creates a non-discretionary demand floor of 800+ new companies per year.

New supply enters ADGM zone▼

One Maryah Place (late 2028) is the only confirmed Grade A delivery inside the window, and it is expected to arrive largely pre-let. Al Reem itself has zero new Grade A office in the pipeline. Our investment horizon completes as that supply lands.

ADGM regulatory changes▼

ADGM is a strategic Abu Dhabi government initiative backed by sovereign wealth. The office mandate is fundamental to the zone's compliance framework. Any relaxation would undermine ADGM's regulatory credibility - extremely unlikely.

Renovation cost overruns▼

Fixed-price contractor agreements. 10% contingency reserve built into the capital stack. Palm & Peak has completed multiple fit-outs in the UAE with consistent budget adherence.

Exit valuation risk▼

Base case uses conservative 9% exit cap rate. Sensitivity analysis shows positive LP returns even at 12% cap. Multiple exit paths (sale, refi, early exit) provide flexibility. Cash distributions begin from Year 1, reducing dependence on exit pricing.
