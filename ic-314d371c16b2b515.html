ADGM jurisdiction: Al Maryah and Al Reem Islands

Why this is the core of the thesis. A landlord on Reem keeps contractual escalations and open-market renewals; a landlord anywhere else in the emirate does not. The business plan's 5% fixed escalations and open-market pricing on renewal are lawful on Reem today and unlawful on the mainland. A policy measure that is a headwind for every other Abu Dhabi commercial landlord is a relative advantage here.

Three consequences for the owner of this floor: leases register with ADGM and are enforceable in ADGM courts; a Reem address satisfies the ADGM registered-office requirement for every licensee; and ADGM entities are free-zone persons for corporate-tax purposes.

Policy risk, pricedIf rent control were extended to ADGM, the investor IRR falls to 20.8% at 0% escalation and 23.3% at 3%, against 24.9% in the base case.

Four-floor case assumes identical pricing per floor and simultaneous acquisition; in practice floors would be acquired and opened in sequence.
  • Floors come to market. Sky Tower has 474 strata office units; comparable floors have been offered at AED 33-35M; Rice has itself expanded floor by floor in the building.

  • Proposal: subsequent floors on pre-agreed terms under the same agreement, with a right of first refusal for the investor on opportunities Palm & Peak sources in the building.
