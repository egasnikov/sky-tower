6. Reading the numbers

What the base case says

At AED 6.5M the rent absorbs 54% of stabilised revenue. With RICE's own Sky Tower pricing (AED 3,068 / desk / month, weighted across L25 Premium and L34 Business) at the Palm & Peak planning density (70 sq ft gross per desk, 329 capacity desks - the Sky Tower Deliverables sizing), the floor produces a stabilised NOI after fee of AED 3.2M on AED 16.3M of equity: LP IRR 10.0%, 1.94x, AED 15.3M LP profit, payback in year 7 of a 10-year term. Break-even occupancy is 69%. The floor works operationally but not as an investment: the rent sits well above the 35% operator band, and the return depends on holding 95% occupancy for a decade with the rent fixed - at 85% occupancy the IRR is 4.3%, at a 5% rent review it is 2.9%, on a 7-year term it is 0.9%. At the Jul 2026 negotiation rate of AED 3,200 / desk the base is 12.1% / 2.17x.

What it takes to work

Holding RICE pricing and 70 sq ft / desk, the rent that delivers a 15% LP IRR is about AED 5.3M; 20% needs AED 4.1M. At AED 6.5M the desk rate needed for 15% is about AED 3,400 (3,396) - inside RICE Premium territory (3,242-3,415), with the whole floor priced as Premium. Twelve months rent-free instead of six adds 3 points (13.0%); a landlord fit-out contribution that brings CapEx to 400 / sq ft adds 4 points (14.2%). Note the density assumption itself: RICE achieves 65 sq ft / desk inside suites but sells only 68% of its Sky Tower plates as suites, so on gross area it runs ~95 - at that density this deal is loss-making (see the density table above).

Negotiation position

The floor is investable at a lower rent or as a landlord partnership. In order of preference: (1) management agreement or rev-share with the owner - Palm & Peak fits out and operates, the owner takes a share of NOI instead of a fixed rent; (2) fixed rent at AED 4.0-4.5M (AED 175-195 / sq ft, still at the top of the building's fitted asks of 125-231; 18-20% LP IRR at base inputs) on a 10-year fixed term with 9-12 months rent-free and a landlord fit-out contribution of AED 200-250 / sq ft; (3) AED 5.0-5.3M fixed as the walk-away line (15-16%). AED 6.5M for a shell floor in Sky Tower is a purchase yield of 14% on the AED 46M ask for the same plate - the landlord is pricing it as if it were already a serviced product.

Reference points

RICE bills AED 571 / sq ft on suite area in Sky Tower, about AED 4,180 / sq m on gross floor area, in a building where fitted full floors ask AED 1,350-2,500 / sq m - a 1.7-3.1x gross-to-net spread. This offer asks for AED 3,042 / sq m against the same revenue: a 1.4x spread, below any flex operator's floor. This model at 70 sq ft / desk implies AED 5,661 / sq m gross - between RICE's actual gross yield and the AED 6,458 / sq m the previous Palm & Peak Sky Tower memos assumed; the memo figure is RICE's suite-area rate applied to the whole plate.
