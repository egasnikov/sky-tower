Operating Build-Up (AED, lease years)

Lease year 1 carries the fit-out period (head rent after the rent-free months, service charge and pre-opening payroll with no revenue) and the occupancy ramp. Leasing commissions of 10% of contracted annual rent fall in the trading years when member leases originate or renew (3-year cycle). Core operating costs (payroll, utilities, pantry, maintenance, insurance) are the Sky Tower Floor 38 budget for this floor size and escalate 3% a year.

What the tenant pays if they do it themselves (AED / sqm / yr)

Conventional fitted space in Sky Tower: full floors ask AED 125-231 / sq ft (1,350-2,500 / sqm), 5-7k sq ft units 139-186 / sq ft (Property Finder and Bayut, Sep 2026). The DIY stack uses the top of the range - the quality a serviced tenant compares against - and the same fit-out basis as this model, amortised over 5 years.

Method and assumptions. Monthly engine, 23,000 sq ft lettable area (2,137 sqm). Head lease at AED 6.5M in year 1 (AED 283/sq ft, AED 3,042/sqm), 6 months rent-free from handover, 5% annual escalation on the head rent; desk rate also escalates 5% a year; service charge from day one; security deposit returned at term end. Fit-out AED 550/sq ft plus 5% contingency, 9 months; 5% VAT on fit-out funded at closing and recovered in lease year 1. Set-up, legal and launch marketing AED 690,000. Two months of pre-opening payroll. Trading opens at 60% of capacity desks, rising 5 points a month to stabilisation; 1% credit loss. Capacity 70 sq ft of lettable area per desk (Sky Tower planning standard). Core operating costs AED 105,100 a month at 2026 prices (Sky Tower Floor 38 budget: payroll, utilities, pantry, maintenance, insurance and admin), 3% escalation; ADGM licence AED 50,000 a year. Leasing commission 10% of contracted annual rent on origination and each 3-year renewal. Operator: 5% management fee on collected revenue, then a 70/30 investor/operator split of each year's positive NOI after fee (no loss carry-forward); the investor funds any negative year through a working-capital reserve sized at closing. Returns are pre-tax and unlevered; no residual value is assumed at lease expiry unless the optional business-sale control is switched on.
